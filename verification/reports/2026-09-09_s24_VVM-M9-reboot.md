## Procedure

1. Built `assembleDebug` and `assembleDebugAndroidTest` from the source baseline
   and installed the debug application with retained app data.
2. Started a Running activity through the production UI and authoritative
   location foreground service.
3. Walked with the phone until durable real GNSS positions and Running step
   samples were present.
4. Captured the source/session state and rebooted the phone while the activity
   remained `RECORDING`.
5. After Android boot and manual unlock, inspected the database before launching
   RADM, then cold-launched the application.
6. Verified the recovery presentation, selected Resume, and walked again to
   produce post-reboot positions and step samples.
7. Inspected identity, events, active time, route segments, counter epochs, and
   source counts, then finished and saved the recovered activity.

## VVM-RECOV-005 — Device Reboot Recovery

The foreground service was active before reboot. A pre-reboot inspection at
14:56:40 CEST showed a durable checkpoint with 183,615 ms active time, 103
positions, and 118 step samples. The service completed one final checkpoint while
the reboot command was taking effect. Before RADM was launched after boot, the
on-disk database contained:

| Field | Recovered value |
|---|---:|
| Durable state | `RECORDING` |
| Active elapsed time | 193,759 ms |
| Last checkpoint | 2026-09-09 14:56:46.249 CEST |
| Positions | 113 |
| Step samples | 118 |
| Current route segment | 1 |
| Events | `START` only |

RADM was not running before the explicit post-boot launch. The cold launch showed
the interrupted-activity recovery screen instead of normal navigation and did not
start the recording service automatically. It presented 03:13 retained active
time, 0.08 km, 113 positions, and 118 step samples.

Resume occurred at 15:00:13.940 CEST, 207.691 seconds after the durable checkpoint,
but the `RECOVERY_RESUME` event retained exactly 193,759 ms active time. The
powered-off and recovery-decision interval was therefore not added to active time.

Result: PASS.

- unresolved activity was detected;
- pre-reboot durable GNSS and step source data remained;
- the powered-off interval was excluded from active elapsed time.

## VVM-RECOV-006 — Reboot Resume

Resume retained the same activity UUID and atomically produced:

- `RECOVERY_RESUME` at active elapsed time 193,759 ms;
- route-segment advancement from 1 to 2;
- the first accepted post-reboot position in segment 2 at 221,827 ms;
- the first post-reboot Running step sample in counter epoch 1 at 207,443 ms.

The resumed evidence snapshot contained 157 positions spanning segments 0..2 and
174 step samples spanning epochs 0..1. This demonstrates that neither route
distance nor step deltas are joined across the reboot discontinuity.

Result: PASS.

- original activity UUID remained;
- a new route segment began;
- `RECOVERY_RESUME` was durable;
- the Running step epoch reset;
- active time continued from the durable pre-reboot checkpoint.

## Final Resolution

The recovered activity was finished and saved through the production UI. Final
database inspection showed:

- active duration 311,740 ms;
- 167 retained positions;
- 182 retained step samples across epochs 0..1;
- `START`, `RECOVERY_RESUME`, and `FINISH` events;
- all five derived processor states `CURRENT`;
- zero unresolved recording sessions.

The final route included an additional normal location-gap segment after the
required recovery segment; no source data was rewritten or fabricated.
