## Production foreground-service and Room field check

The explicit opt-in test
`AndroidStepSourceFieldTest.vvmField006_realRunningPersistsIncreasingPhoneStepCounterSamples`
was launched directly on the paired reference device with
`radmFieldStep=true`.

During the 45-second movement window:

- Running was started through the production foreground recording service;
- the production `TYPE_STEP_COUNTER` listener was registered with
  `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION` granted;
- the operator was asked to carry the phone for a short, approximately 30-step
  controlled movement segment;
- Finish stopped acquisition and flushed the source stream through the normal
  controller/Room transaction;
- 18 cumulative source events were retained;
- the observed comparable within-epoch delta was 17 steps;
- the first retained cumulative value was treated as the baseline and therefore
  was not counted as steps performed from zero;
- the test passed and cleaned up its recording session.

Android `sensorservice` independently identified the source as Samsung's
`android.sensor.step_counter` with the required
`android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`, and recorded successful registration
by `com.jeppe.radm.platform.steps.AndroidStepSource`.

Result: PASS (`VVM-FIELD-006`) for source integration. R00 defines no strict
absolute consumer-sensor accuracy threshold; this short check does not establish
a broader accuracy claim.

## Notes on reruns

An initial permission-denial test attempted to revoke the target application's
permission from inside its own instrumentation process. Android correctly killed
that process, so the test was changed to inject denial at the adapter boundary;
the controller separately injects the resulting `SecurityException`. A later
connected run reached an existing Activity recreation case while the device was
dozing behind its lockscreen. After waking/dismissing the test-device keyguard,
the complete connected suite passed. Neither interrupted run is reported as a
pass.
